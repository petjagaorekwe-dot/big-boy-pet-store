About Big Boy Pet Store

A fictional small-business concept created for the E-Business practical assignment.

Our story

Big Boy Pet Store is an online pet-care shop designed to make everyday shopping easier for busy South African pet owners. The store brings together food, treats, toys, beds and accessories in one clear digital catalogue.

Our mission is to provide a convenient, friendly and trustworthy online shopping experience while helping customers find practical products for the pets they love.

What makes us different?

We focus on a simple customer journey: clear categories, useful descriptions, visible prices, easy add-to-cart actions and straightforward checkout information. The store is designed to work well on both desktop and mobile screens.

Target market: South African dog and cat owners, especially students, young professionals and families who value convenience and accessible pet-care products.
